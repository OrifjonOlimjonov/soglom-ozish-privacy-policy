Privacy Policy for Soglomozish

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Soglomozish ("the app", "we", "us") is a health and wellness app that helps you track meals, water intake, daily steps and weight goals. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address (used as your username) and password. We use these to create your account and sign you in.
  • Profile information: age, date of birth, gender, height and weight. We use these to calculate your body mass index (BMI) and to show your progress toward your weight goal.
  • Meals and water intake: the foods and drinks you log, and your daily calorie and water statistics.

Information collected from your device

  • Step count: with your permission (Android ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION), the app reads your device's built-in step counter sensor to show your daily step count on the home screen. We do not use this permission to detect any other activity, and we do not collect your location.
  • Notifications: with your permission, the app shows reminders (for example, to drink water or log a meal). Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: your logged meals, water intake, step count, goal settings and app preferences (such as theme and notification settings) are stored locally on your device. Your step count is not sent to our servers.
  • On our server: your account and profile information (name, email, password, age, date of birth, gender, height, weight) is stored on our server at api.soglomozish.uz. All communication between the app and the server uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • create and secure your account and sign you in;
  • confirm your email address with a one-time code sent to it;
  • calculate and display your BMI, calorie, water, step and goal statistics;
  • send the reminders you have enabled;
  • operate, maintain and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • when required by law or a valid legal request;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the app.

5. Health information

The app is a wellness and tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical decisions. Some features, such as AI analysis, are still in development and are not currently available. If we release them, we will update this policy before they collect or process any additional data.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile → delete account). When you do, your account and the profile data stored on our server are deleted. Data stored locally on your device (meals, water, steps, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Delete your account

You can delete your Soglomozish account and its data in either of the following ways.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot access the app, send a request from the email address linked to your account to orifjon.olimjonov@mail.ru with the subject "Delete my Soglomozish account". We will delete your account and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile information stored on our server: name, email, password, age, date of birth, gender, height and weight.
  • Data stored only on your device (meals, water intake, steps, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect data in transit and take reasonable steps to protect the data stored on our server. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile information in the app, and delete your account at any time. You can also contact us to access, correct or delete your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version at this location and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
